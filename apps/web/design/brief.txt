Supercal web dashboard, app shell with the Calendars screen as the example content. Supercal is a self-hosted daemon that aggregates many calendar accounts (Google, Microsoft 365, iCloud, CalDAV, ICS links) into one feed any calendar app can subscribe to; users choose which calendars are published, merge several into virtual calendars, and set a privacy level per calendar (full, title-only, busy-only). Audience: technical individuals and small teams who self-host. Brand idea: "one calm view of many calendars". Quiet, precise, information-dense like a good calendar app, never a marketing site.
Shell: left sidebar with nav items Overview, Accounts, Calendars, Preview, Subscribe, Settings, Admin; a tenant switcher at the top of the sidebar; a small "Live" status indicator; user menu. Main area: "Calendars" heading, a feed picker ("My calendars"), and calendars grouped by account (e.g. "juan@work.example · Google", "iCloud"), each row with a colour chip, name, a publish toggle, a privacy badge (Full / Title only / Busy only) and freshness text like "synced 2 min ago". One row shows a chip "in: Work+Personal".
Must keep copy exactly: "Supercal", "Calendars", "My calendars".
Must not: marketing hero, stock photos, illustrations of people, gradients for decoration, emoji.
Must work in light and dark themes; calendar colours come from user data.
